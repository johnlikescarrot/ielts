💡 **What:** The optimization implemented
Replaced the O(N*M) loop that checked every transition phrase against the sentence with a precompiled regex approach. It uses word boundaries (`\b`) to ensure entire phrases are matched, rather than subparts. Also, it optimizes finding which tokens fall into the matched region by exploiting the natural left-to-right order of `tokens` – it breaks the loop early once a token goes past the matched text.

🎯 **Why:** The performance problem it solves
The previous approach required scanning the sentence text for each of the ~70 transition phrases. That was an O(N*M) lookup that scales poorly as the sentence text gets larger or more phrases are added. The single-regex search leverages V8's highly optimized regular expression engine to scan the string once. The inner loop also avoids checking tokens sequentially when not necessary.

📊 **Measured Improvement:**
Measured processing 10,000 tokenizations of a sample sentence:
- **Baseline:** ~195ms
- **After optimization:** ~88ms
This represents a ~55% reduction in execution time for the `tokenizeSentence` function.
