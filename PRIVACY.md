# Bandcraft privacy policy

**Effective date:** 27 September 2026

Bandcraft does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data, browsing activity, study content, audio, telemetry, or diagnostics.

- Study data is stored only in Firefox's local extension storage.
- Page text is saved only after the user explicitly chooses **Save selection to Bandcraft**.
- Microphone audio is processed in the extension page and is not uploaded.
- Imported audio and transcript files remain in the current browser session.
- Bandcraft has no analytics, advertising, account system, remote API, or tracking code.
- Exporting a backup creates a file chosen and controlled by the user.
- Removing the extension normally removes its Firefox-managed extension data.

The manifest declares `data_collection_permissions.required: ["none"]`.

Questions and security reports can be filed through the repository's issue tracker. Please do not include private study content in public reports.
